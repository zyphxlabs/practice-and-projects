# Home Asset Inventory Lab - Google Cybersecurity Certificate (Course 5)

## What I did

For this lab I had to build out a home asset inventory for a small business running out of a home office. The scenario had a desktop computer printer router webcam speakers headphones and external hard drive all connected to the same home network. My job was to pick three devices that were not already listed and add them in along with characteristics like network access owner and location then classify each one by sensitivity.

## What I found

The network router was already filled in as an example and it made sense once I looked at it closer. It has continuous access since its always on and connects through the ISP. It runs both a 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz connection and every device on the home network connects through the 5 GHz frequency. I classified this as confidential since access is limited to specific users on that network.

The desktop was occasional access owned by the homeowner and kept on premises. Since it holds private information like photos I marked this restricted meaning its need to know only. This one felt like the most personal device out of the whole inventory.

The guest smartphone was interesting because it goes on and off premises depending on if the friend is visiting or not. I labeled this internal only since it does connect to the home network but the access isnt really controlled the same way as the owners own devices.

After this when I came to the part where I had to add my own three devices that was when things started to get more detailed. I added the external hard drive the streaming media player and the portable game console.

The external hard drive was occasional access owned by the homeowner and stayed on premises. It only holds music and movies so I classified it confidential since its still tied to the home network but doesnt carry anything as sensitive as the desktop.

The streaming media player had continuous access and was on premises the whole time. This one stood out to me because it stores payment card information for movie rentals. I classified it internal only but honestly the more I sat with it the more I felt like this should get looked at again since payment data sitting on a device like that is not something to take lightly.

The portable game console was occasional and moved on and off premises with the friend. It has a camera and microphone built in which I noted since that could be a privacy concern if the device isnt secured properly. I classified this internal only as well.

## Sensitivity classification summary

- Network router - confidential
- Desktop - restricted
- Guest smartphone - internal-only
- External hard drive - confidential
- Streaming media player - internal-only
- Portable game console - internal-only

## Notes on what stood out

The biggest thing I took away from this lab is that not every device on a home network carries the same level of risk even though they are all technically connected to the same router. The desktop and the streaming media player were the two that made me pause the most since one holds personal photos and the other is holding payment card information which needs the most urgent attention out of everything in this inventory.
